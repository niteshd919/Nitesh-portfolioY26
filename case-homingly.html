01The first wallProblem

Everyone wants a beautiful home. Almost no one knows what one costs.

Finding the right products for a room is hard, and most people can’t afford a design firm that charges high fees for consultation and renovation.

What we heard

People don’t know what a renovation should cost, so they overspend, stall, or never start.

The price comes before every other question: before the style, before the products, before choosing who to trust with the work. Quotes that change after a site visit and design firms asking ₹50k or more up front teach people to stay away.

02A door in the wallThe bet

Answer “what will it cost?” first, and hiring a designer becomes a next step.

Homingly puts three things on one platform: curated products at fair prices, a renovation estimate in minutes, and vetted interior designers who guide the work. Designers get portfolios that find them projects. The estimate comes first.

04House rulesPrinciples

Four rules every screen had to keep.

Each rule is a constraint on the design, shown here on a screen that keeps it.

  1. Principle 01

    Value before sign-up

    Show what Homingly does before asking for a number. The welcome screen sells the outcome; sign-in asks for one field.

  2. Principle 02

    Honest numbers

    Prices appear as ranges with their assumptions, never a teaser number that grows later. Fees and taxes show before payment.

  3. Principle 03

    One decision per screen

    The estimator is split into small, reversible steps, with a visible progress bar and sensible defaults.

  4. Principle 04

    Creative, calm, clear

    Warm photography and a serif display face make browsing feel creative. Every action stays high-contrast, labelled and within thumb reach.

07Honest numbersPricing

Every price shows its working.

Wherever money appears, the app gives a range or a breakdown and says what it assumes. The total is always on screen before a pay button.

  1. Note 01A range, never a teaser₹3.4L – ₹4.1L for the whole job, materials and labour.
  2. Note 02What it assumesRoom, size and finish on top; the city’s rates and the month they were set below.
  3. Note 03Where the money goesFour lines, each with its own share and its own range.
  4. Note 04Who confirms itA designer confirms the exact quote after a site visit.
  5. Note 05What nextFind designers for this project, or save it and come back.

Where the money goes, as ranges

Money moves after the work does

08Every wall has a doorEdge cases

Thirteen ways it can go wrong, and a way back from each.

Every error says what happened in plain words, says whether money moved, and puts the fix on the main button. Twice the screen has to say it about a payment, and twice it says the same thing: no money was taken.

Flow 01 · Book a designer7 states

  1. CheckValid number?

    Sign in · Invalid number

    “Enter a valid 10-digit mobile number”

    Way backFix the number. Send code stays off until it’s valid.

  2. CheckCode correct?

    Verify · Wrong code

    “That code didn’t match.”

    Way backRe-enter the code, or have it sent again.

  3. CheckToo many misses?

    Verify · Too many attempts

    “For your security, sign-in is paused for 10 minutes.”

    Way backA countdown on the button, and Get help.

  4. CheckOnline?

    Home · Offline

    “We’ll refresh this page as soon as you’re back online.”

    Way backTry again. Saved projects still open.

  5. CheckDesigners match?

    Matches · No designers

    “Try removing a filter or widening your budget.”

    Way backClear all filters, or get notified when one is available.

  6. CheckSlot still free?

    Pick a slot · Slot just taken

    “11:30 AM was just booked by someone else.”

    Way backPick another time. The taken slot stays visible, greyed out.

  7. CheckPayment OK?

    Review & pay · Payment failed

    “Payment of ₹1,770 failed. No money was taken.”

    Way backRetry payment, or change the method.

Flow 02 · Shop the look6 states

  1. CheckAny results?

    Category · No results

    “Metal floor lamps start around ₹2,400.”

    Way backClear the filters, or show lamps under ₹3,000.

  2. CheckIn stock?

    Product · Out of stock

    “Blush is back around 5 Nov.”

    Way backNotify me when it’s back, or pick a finish that ships now.

  3. CheckItems in the bag?

    Bag · Empty

    “You have 4 pieces saved in your wishlist.”

    Way backShop the Diwali edit, or open the wishlist.

  4. CheckAll available?

    Bag · Item out of stock

    “Remove it to continue — your offers have been updated.”

    Way backRemove the item. Offers are recalculated on the spot.

  5. CheckPincode served?

    Delivery · Not serviceable

    “The jute rug can’t be delivered to 560102 yet.”

    Way backChange the address, or remove only the rug.

  6. CheckPayment OK?

    Payment · Bank declined

    “Your bank declined ₹17,130. No money was taken.”

    Way backRetry, or choose another method below.

Four colours, darkened until they passed

Four Tailwind colours failed WCAG AA as text on white. Each was darkened, with its hue kept, until it cleared 4.5 : 1. Five more were added where a pair fell short: gray/450 for input borders, yellow/600 for rating stars, blue/300 and red/300 for Dark-mode text, and green/400 kept for Dark-mode success.

24 of 24 pairs pass AA, in Light and in Dark

Text needs 4.5 : 1; borders, icons and progress need 3 : 1. I recomputed every ratio from the live tokens, blending Dark mode’s translucent fills over their surface first. The closest call is a filled input’s border, at 3.03 against 3.

11Still to proveResearch next

What I would test before calling it finished.

The design rests on four assumptions. None of them is proven yet, and each one could change a screen.

  1. Assumption 01

    Phone and OTP is the sign-in Indian users prefer over email and password.

  2. Assumption 02

    People trust a price range more than a single number.

  3. Assumption 03

    Sharing the estimate with the designer makes the consultation better for both sides.

  4. Assumption 04

    A paid, refundable consult cuts no-shows without hurting conversion.

Show the number first. Every step after it is easier to take.
